Made in Riyadh

Crafted for slow afternoons and quiet Saudi evenings.

In our Diriyah-adjacent studio, each Sanctuary Lounger is individually cut and assembled by master upholsterers. Rather than using generic synthetic canvas, we celebrate honest natural textures—raw linen yarns that soften with every sit and breathable cotton that remains comfortable even during Riyadh’s warmest summer afternoons.

Inspired by the timeless Majlis tradition of low-to-the-earth gathering, reworked through clean Scandinavian lines.
